### 🧠 Research Interests

My work sits at the intersection of **Data-centric AI**, **LLMs**, and **Computer Vision**. I am particularly interested in:

-   **Data-Centric AI:** Understanding data influence, dataset distillation, and data pruning.
-   **LLM Reasoning:** Reinforcement fine-tuning, Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning, and reward attribution.
-   **Multimodal Learning:** Vision-Language pre-training and robust image-text data quality.
